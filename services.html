Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with XDG Software as your technology partner.

Do we own the source code and Intellectual Property?

Yes, 100%. Upon completion and payment, all custom code, design assets, database schemas, and documentation belong completely to your company with zero vendor lock-in.

Can you integrate with our existing legacy systems?

Absolutely. We specialize in building secure API adapters, data synchronization scripts, and modern UI wrappers around legacy databases and third-party tools.

Do we need a full technical specification before contacting you?

No. You only need to explain your business process, where friction exists, or what outcome you need. Our Discovery phase translates that into clear technical architecture.

What happens after the initial system is launched?

We provide warranty support after deployment, and we offer long-term partnership agreements to continuously evolve the software as your users and operational requirements grow.
